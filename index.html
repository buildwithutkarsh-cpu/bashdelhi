A weekend to make something you’re proud of.

BashDelhi is a two-day in-person hackathon for school and college students to create, experiment, collaborate and show off their technical creativity.

It is free and beginners are welcome. Join with a team of two to four, and learn by building something real.

Pick your track

Four tracks, one weekend. Beginners are welcome in all of them.

Web

Create websites, web apps and digital solutions that tackle real problems or explore creative ideas.

Game

Design and build games, interactive experiences and playful projects with the tools you choose.

Agentic AI

Build AI agents that plan, use tools and take actions to get real tasks done.

Hardware

Bring your own components and build something you can touch. We do not supply kits.

Why BashDelhi

Open to all

Technology and hackathons should be accessible to every student, whatever their experience.

Hands-on

Creativity, problem solving and practical projects, not just theory.

Together

Connect with student developers across schools and colleges, and learn from each other’s projects.

How the weekend runs

Two days, start to finish, in person. Timings and the venue will be published once confirmed.

1

Opening ceremony

The hackathon begins and everyone gets the details.

2

Teams and ideas

Settle your team of two to four, pick a track and shape your idea.

3

Development

Design and code your project with the tools you prefer.

4

Testing

Fix bugs, polish the details and get it ready to share.

5

Submissions

Submit your project.

6

Judging and results

Projects are reviewed, results are announced and the community sees what everyone built.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Taking part is free.

Where does it happen?

In person. The venue is still to be announced.

When is it?

It is a two-day event on 5 and 6 December 2026, subject to confirmation of the organising team’s availability. Final schedules, rules, registration and judging details will be published once confirmed.

Who can take part?

School and college students, including beginners.

What can I build?

Choose one of four tracks: web development, game development, agentic AI or hardware.

How does the hardware track work?

It is bring your own components. Teams bring their own parts, boards and tools, and we do not supply kits. More details will be shared once confirmed.

Do I need a team?

Yes. Each team has two to four participants.

Are there prizes, certificates or mentors?

Digital certificates and other recognition will follow published eligibility criteria. Prizes, mentors and partnerships will be announced only when confirmed.
